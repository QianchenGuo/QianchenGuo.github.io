Studying [stochastic optimal estimation](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%9A%8F%E6%9C%BA%E6%9C%80%E4%BC%98%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1&zhida_source=entity) has four levels of depth:

1. The relationships among five estimators—minimum variance estimation, [linear minimum variance estimation](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%BA%BF%E6%80%A7%E6%9C%80%E5%B0%8F%E6%96%B9%E5%B7%AE%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1&zhida_source=entity), maximum likelihood estimation, maximum a posteriori estimation, and [Bayes estimation](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=bayes%E4%BC%B0%E8%AE%A1&zhida_source=entity)—along with their equivalence conditions and proofs;

2. Applying the five estimators above to first-order Markov [state-space](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%8A%B6%E6%80%81%E7%A9%BA%E9%97%B4&zhida_source=entity) models, under certain conditions and assumptions, and deriving [Kalman filtering](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=Kalman%E6%BB%A4%E6%B3%A2&zhida_source=entity) from five perspectives within the probabilistic framework;

3. Stepping outside the probabilistic framework to unify the least squares and Kalman filtering from the [stochastic system control](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%9A%8F%E6%9C%BA%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F%E6%8E%A7%E5%88%B6&zhida_source=entity) viewpoint with the least squares and Kalman observer from the deterministic system control viewpoint—even unifying the minimum variance criterion in the probabilistic sense with the [LQR optimal criterion](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=LQR%E6%9C%80%E4%BC%98%E5%87%86%E5%88%99&zhida_source=entity)—and identifying the similarities and differences between the two perspectives;

4. Venturing further into nonlinear systems, comparing the design of nonlinear filtering with [nonlinear observers](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%9D%9E%E7%BA%BF%E6%80%A7%E8%A7%82%E6%B5%8B%E5%99%A8&zhida_source=entity), and finding their commonalities and differences to complete a unified picture of observers under both deterministic and probabilistic frameworks. These can even inform and advance each other, especially since proving the stability of nonlinear filtering within the probabilistic framework remains an open problem.

The first two parts of this article correspond to items 1 and 2 above; for items 3 and 4, I will make time to attempt building this bridge. I also believe that studying optimal estimation is highly beneficial for learning traditional machine learning (whether statistical or Bayesian), since both are underpinned by mathematical statistics. Mastering these topics also lays a solid foundation for later study of [information geometry](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%BF%A1%E6%81%AF%E5%87%A0%E4%BD%95&zhida_source=entity).

## 3. Issues with Bayes Filtering for Nonlinear Systems and the Gauss Filtering Framework

For [nonlinear systems](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=3&q=%E9%9D%9E%E7%BA%BF%E6%80%A7%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F&zhida_source=entity), Bayes filtering encounters the problem of having no [closed-form solution](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%97%AD%E5%BC%8F%E8%A7%A3&zhida_source=entity). This stems from nonlinearity and non-Gaussianity, which are strongly coupled.

![](/blog-assets/zhihu/721626051/image-14.jpg)



![](/blog-assets/zhihu/721626051/image-16.jpg)



![](/blog-assets/zhihu/721626051/image-18.jpg)

We can observe that within the nonlinear Gaussian filtering framework, the extended Kalman filter, statistically linearized filter, unscented Kalman filter, Gauss–Hermite filter, and cubature Kalman filter (where the latter three are also known as [sigma-point Kalman filters](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=248491681&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=sigma%E7%82%B9Kalman%E6%BB%A4%E6%B3%A2&zhida_source=entity)) can be elegantly unified.
